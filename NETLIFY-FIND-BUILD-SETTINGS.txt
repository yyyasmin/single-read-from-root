================================================================================
NETLIFY: HOW TO FIND BUILD SETTINGS
================================================================================

STEP 1 - Open your site
  Go to: https://app.netlify.com
  Click on your site name: single-read-from-root-frontend

STEP 2 - Open the menu
  Look at the LEFT SIDEBAR. You should see:
    - Site overview
    - Deploys
    - Build & deploy    <-- CLICK THIS
    - and more...

STEP 3 - Under "Build & deploy"
  After clicking "Build & deploy", a submenu may open. Look for:
    - Continuous deployment   <-- CLICK THIS (or "Build settings")
  That page shows: Branch, Base directory, Build command, Publish directory.
  Click "Edit settings" or "Configure" or "Options" to change them.

ALTERNATIVE PATH (newer Netlify):
  Left menu -> "Site configuration" -> "Build & deploy" -> "Continuous deployment"
  Then click "Edit settings" or the pencil icon next to the build settings.

STEP 4 - If you still don't see it
  Left menu -> "Site configuration" (or "Configuration")
  Then look for "Build settings" or "Build & deploy" on that page.

STEP 5 - Change the values
  Base directory:  type  frontend
  Publish directory:  type  dist
  Save.

ENVIRONMENT VARIABLE:
  Left menu -> "Environment variables" (under Build & deploy)
  Or: Site configuration -> Environment variables
  Click "Add a variable" or "Add environment variable" -> Add "VITE_API_URL".
