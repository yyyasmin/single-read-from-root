================================================================================
RENDER - WHAT TO CHOOSE FOR A FREE ACCOUNT
================================================================================

When it asks:

  "What would you like to call your workspace?"
  -> Type anything, e.g.  My workspace  (or leave "My workspace").
  Optional, no effect on free tier.

  "What will you use this workspace for?"
  -> Choose:  Personal projects
  (Free tier is for personal/small use.)

  "How many developers (including yourself) will be working together?"
  -> Choose:  Just me

Then continue. That keeps you on the free tier. You can create a Web Service
and use the free plan (service may sleep after inactivity but will work).
